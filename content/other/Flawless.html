2008 Upper Deck Exquisite Basketball Flawless Autographs

A Historic Showcase of NBA Greatness & Rare Autographs

The Peak of Upper Deck's "Exquisite" Era

This gallery presents an exceptional collection of 2008 Upper Deck Exquisite Flawless basketball cards. As the final "Exquisite" release from Upper Deck under the NBA license, this set holds a legendary status in the hobby, featuring some of the most iconic players in basketball history.

From the iconic Michael Jordan (/23) and Bill Russell (/25) to modern-day greats like LeBron James, Kobe Bryant, and Kevin Durant, this collection is a tribute to the "Flawless" design that helped shape the future of high-end sports card collecting. Each card features on-card autographs and a level of elegance that remains the gold standard for many sports enthusiasts.
