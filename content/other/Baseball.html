About the Baseball Collection

This specialized gallery at Maulmann Trading Cards focuses on a rare assembly of 2005 Upper Deck Ultimate Signature Edition cards. The hallmark of this collection is the "Immortal Inscriptions" set, where baseball's greatest icons have added personal, hand-written notes and legendary nicknames alongside their authentic signatures.

From the precise "Game Over" inscription of Eric Gagne to the Hall of Fame legacy of Ozzie Smith, these cards represent a unique intersection of sports history and high-end memorabilia. We also feature historic buy-back autographs, including icons like Ken Griffey Jr., preserving the timeless appeal of the diamond's greatest stars.
